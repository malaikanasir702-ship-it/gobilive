Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining as a streamer or managing an agency.

How do host diamond payouts work? ▼
Hosts earn Diamonds whenever gifts are sent in their audio/video rooms. Diamonds can be exchanged back to cash and withdrawn via connected Stripe / Bank accounts automatically once reaching the minimum payout threshold.
How do I create and manage a host agency? ▼
Agencies can register directly within the app dashboard. Upon approval, you will receive an Agency Code to invite hosts, monitor their daily live stream hours, and earn commissions on their diamond earnings.
Is live streaming supported on Android and iOS? ▼
Yes, GlobiLive is developed in Flutter, allowing smooth cross-platform performance across both Android and iOS devices using low-latency Agora RTC servers.
